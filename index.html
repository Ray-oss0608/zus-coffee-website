About Us

A Homegrown Brand with a Simple Promise

ZUS Coffee started in Malaysia in 2019 with one idea: everyone deserves a really good cup of coffee, every single day. Instead of making premium coffee feel exclusive, we focus on consistent quality, friendly service and fair prices.

Today our deep blue cups are a familiar sight on campuses, in offices and on busy city streets. Whether you need a quick takeaway between classes or a quiet corner to study, ZUS is designed around the way young Malaysians live.
